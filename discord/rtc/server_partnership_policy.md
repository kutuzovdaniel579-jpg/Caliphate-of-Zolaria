# 📜 Risers Of The Citadel — Partnership Policy

Welcome to the official partnership program of **Citadel**. We believe in mutual growth, collaboration, and building a secure, engaging network with other communities.

Please review our requirements and guidelines carefully before applying for a partnership.

## 📌 Partnership Requirements

To ensure a safe and high-quality environment for both communities, partner servers must meet the following baseline requirements:

1. **Active Community Base:**

   * A minimum of **7 active members** (bots do not count toward this total).

   * Regular daily chat activity and organic engagement.

2. **Security & Verification (Mandatory):**

   * Some sort of **verification system** must be implemented for all incoming members to prevent raids, spam, and optionally bot accounts.

3. **Content & Safety:**

   * Zero tolerance for NSFW, illegal content, hate speech, or toxic behavior.

   * A well-moderated staff team that actively handles issues.

4. **Mutual Promotion:**

   * Willingness to host our permanent invite link in your designated partner/promo channel.

   * Both servers must maintain each other's links actively (removing our link without notice will result in partnership termination).

## 🚫 Grounds for Rejection or Termination

Your partnership application may be denied, or an existing partnership may be terminated, under the following circumstances:

* **Fake Stats:** Using self-bots, raid tools, or buying fake members/reactions.

* **Toxicity:** If the partner server promotes toxicity, drama, or raids against other communities.

* **Inactivity:** If the server becomes completely inactive or shuts down.

* **Rule Violations:** Breaking Discord's Terms of Service (ToS) or Community Guidelines.

## 📩 How to Apply

If your server meets all the requirements above and you wish to partner with **RTC / Citadel**:

1. Open a **support ticket** in our \[server\](https://discord.gg/GnrQv2VmyM).

2. Provide your server's **permanent invite link**, member count, and a brief description of your community.

3. Our staff team will review your application and get back to you shortly.

*Thank you for your interest in growing together with Risers Of The Citadel!*
